# HireAI – AI-Powered Recruitment & Applicant Tracking System

HireAI is a full-stack, AI-powered recruitment management platform built to simplify and streamline the hiring process. It provides dedicated Candidate and Recruiter Portals to manage job opportunities, resumes, applications, candidate profiles, and AI-based resume matching.

The platform connects job seekers with recruiters through an organized recruitment workflow while helping recruiters evaluate applications more efficiently using AI-powered insights.

## 🌐 Live Application

- **Live Portal:** https://hireai-7193fd.netlify.app/
- **GitHub Repository:** https://github.com/Adarshpakala2001/hireai

## ✨ Key Features

### 👨‍💻 Candidate Portal
- Secure candidate registration and login.
- Browse available job opportunities.
- Upload and manage resumes.
- Apply for relevant job openings.
- Track application status.
- Manage personal and professional profiles.
- View AI-powered resume matching results.
- Identify matching and missing skills.

### 🏢 Recruiter Portal
- Dedicated recruiter registration and login.
- Recruiter dashboard with recruitment statistics.
- Create, publish, edit, and manage job postings.
- Browse and review candidate profiles.
- Access candidate resumes and application details.
- View applications received for job postings.
- Review AI-generated resume matching insights.
- Update application statuses and manage recruitment activities.

### 🤖 AI-Powered Resume Analysis
- Analyze resumes against job descriptions.
- Generate resume match scores.
- Identify matching and missing skills.
- Evaluate experience alignment.
- Provide candidate strengths and improvement suggestions.

## 🏗️ Application Architecture

HireAI is organized into two primary workspaces:

**1. Candidate Workspace**
- Registration and authentication
- Job discovery
- Resume management
- Job applications
- AI resume analysis
- Candidate profile management

**2. Recruiter Workspace**
- Recruiter authentication
- Recruitment dashboard
- Job management
- Candidate management
- Application management
- Candidate evaluation and AI matching insights

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Simplify the recruitment and applicant tracking process.
- Reduce manual resume screening efforts.

- Provide dedicated workspaces for candidates and recruiters.
- Centralize job postings, resumes, and applications.
